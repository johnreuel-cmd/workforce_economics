# Workforce Economics: Hiring vs. Retention Investment Analysis

> ## Northstar is growing — but is its workforce investment keeping pace with that growth?

Northstar's leadership needs to balance **three competing workforce costs**:

**Hiring new employees** to support growth  
**Retaining existing employees** to avoid costly turnover  
**Managing vacancies** that create economic exposure while positions remain unfilled

### Executive Question

> **Where should Northstar allocate its next workforce investment: hiring, retention, or both?**

---

## 1. Background / Context

**Northstar Digital Services** is a growing Philippine-based technology and
business-services company with approximately **1,000 employees** across its
departments.

As the company expands, leadership is facing a workforce economics challenge:
**hiring demand is increasing, employee turnover is creating replacement
costs, and prolonged vacancies are creating additional economic exposure.**

To support the **Executive Team and People Operations**, this analysis
connects **workforce movement to financial impact** to identify:

- where workforce decisions create the greatest financial exposure
- which roles and departments drive that exposure
- where hiring investment is needed to support growth
- where retention investment may reduce costly employee replacement
- where targeted workforce action can have the greatest business impact

The analysis brings together **hiring investment, vacancy exposure, employee
replacement cost, retention investment, and attrition patterns** into an
executive decision framework.
